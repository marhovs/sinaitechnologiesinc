## 🛠 Key Architecture & Capabilities

SINAI connects granular emissions data with corporate financial planning to transition businesses from static carbon accounting to dynamic decarbonization.

### 1. Audit-Grade Scope 1–3 Carbon Accounting
* **Automated Data Ingestion:** Connect utility data, IoT streams, and enterprise ERP metrics via REST APIs and batch integrations.
* **Calculation Provenance:** Trace every calculated metric back to original activity data, maintaining an audit trail certified against ISO 14064 standards and the GHG Protocol.
* **100,000+ Emission Factor Libraries:** Automated matching across IPCC, DEFRA, EPA, and custom regional factor sets.

### 2. Supply Chain & Scope 3 Data Pipelines
* **Primary Data Collection:** Direct supplier engagement workflows to replace generic spend-based estimates with primary operational data.
* **Hotspot Identification:** Automated AI allocation tools to pinpoint carbon-intensive value chain partners and materials.

### 3. Financial Decarbonization & MACC Modeling
* **Marginal Abatement Cost Curves (MACC):** Evaluate emissions reduction options alongside CapEx, OpEx, net present value (NPV), and payback periods.
* **Internal Carbon Pricing (ICP):** Model scenarios for internal carbon taxation and regulatory financial liabilities.

### 4. Automated Multi-Framework Disclosures
Pre-configured reporting exports for global regulatory and voluntary reporting frameworks:
* **EU CSRD** (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive)
* **EU CBAM** (Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism)
* **California SB 253 & SB 261**
* **CDP, TCFD, SEC, and SBTi alignment**
